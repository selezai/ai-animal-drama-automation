# AI Animal Drama Content Strategy
## Facebook Monetization Blueprint

**Created:** March 2026  
**Goal:** Build a sustainable, monetizable AI content page targeting Facebook's core demographic (30-55) through emotional animal storytelling.

---

## 1. The Core Concept

### Brand Identity: "The Creek" (Working Title)
A recurring cast of anthropomorphic animal characters living in a shared world (a neighborhood, a park, a farm — keeps continuity without requiring episode order).

### Format
- **Length:** 30-60 seconds
- **Structure:** Self-contained emotional stories with recurring characters
- **Style:** Object Talk (AI-voiced internal monologue/dialogue)
- **Posting:** 2x daily, 7-9pm SAST

---

## 2. The Character Bible

### Primary Cast (The Core 4)

**Charlie** — Golden Retriever  
- **Archetype:** The loyal optimist who keeps getting hurt
- **Voice:** Warm, earnest, slightly naive
- **Story roles:** Betrayal victim, forgiveness arc, loyal friend
- **Why he works:** Golden retrievers are universally loved; "sad dog" content performs exceptionally

**Milo** — Tabby Cat  
- **Archetype:** The sarcastic observer with hidden softness
- **Voice:** Dry, witty, defensive
- **Story roles:** Reluctant helper, jealousy plotlines, comic relief with heart
- **Why he works:** Cat lovers are rabid; the "secretly soft" cat is a proven trope

**Bella** — African Grey Parrot  
- **Archetype:** The gossip who reveals secrets
- **Voice:** Fast-talking, dramatic, nosy
- **Story roles:** Catalyst for drama, truth-teller, chaos agent
- **Why she works:** Parrots talking = inherent shareability; the "revealer" drives plots

**Duke** — Bulldog  
- **Archetype:** The gruff protector with a tragic past
- **Voice:** Gravelly, short sentences, emotional when vulnerable
- **Story roles:** Rescue scenes, mentor moments, unexpected kindness
- **Why he works:** Bulldogs have built-in character; "tough but soft" is share catnip

### Supporting Cast (Rotate Monthly)
- **Kitten/Young animal** — for jealousy/protection stories
- **Stray animal** — for rescue/redemption arcs
- **Human owner** (unseen, just referenced) — for "abandoned" or "new baby" plots
- **Vet character** — for illness/recovery emotional beats

## 3. Content Pillars (The 5 Story Types)

### Pillar 1: Betrayal & Loyalty (40% of content)
**Formula:** Character discovers they're second-best → emotional moment → resolution

**Examples:**
- Charlie sees his owner with a new puppy; thinks he's being replaced
- Milo finds out Charlie has been visiting another cat for snacks
- Duke's favorite human brings home a younger, cuter dog

**Hook structure:** "He thought he was the only one..."

### Pillar 2: Unexpected Kindness (25% of content)
**Formula:** Hard exterior → vulnerable moment → someone sees them

**Examples:**
- Bella sees Duke crying and stays quiet instead of gossiping
- Milo shares his warm spot with a shivering stray
- Charlie comforts an animal who was mean to him

**Hook structure:** "Nobody expected him to..."

### Pillar 3: Secrets Revealed (20% of content)
**Formula:** Hidden truth → Bella exposes it → fallout

**Examples:**
- Milo has been sneaking food to a stray; Bella sees it
- Duke's "tough" backstory is revealed
- Charlie's owner has been secretly caring for another animal

**Hook structure:** "The secret he kept for 3 years..."

### Pillar 4: Jealousy & Insecurity (10% of content)
**Formula:** New character arrives → existing character reacts → realization

**Examples:**
- New kitten gets all the attention
- Younger dog can do tricks Charlie can't
- Milo sees his owner petting another cat

**Hook structure:** "She didn't know she was being replaced..."

### Pillar 5: Found Family (5% of content — High emotional payoff)
**Formula:** Isolation → connection → belonging

**Examples:**
- Stray is adopted into the group
- Animals from different species bond
- Duke lets someone into his "pack"

**Hook structure:** "He finally found where he belonged..."

## 4. The Script Formula (Per Episode)

### Scene Structure (45 seconds)
```
0-3s: HOOK — Visual + opening line (character looking at camera)
3-15s: SETUP — Establish the situation, the hope/dream
15-30s: CONFLICT/TWIST — The moment everything changes
30-42s: EMOTIONAL PEAK — The character's reaction, internal monologue
42-45s: RESOLUTION — Either happy ending OR cliffhanger
```

### Dialogue Rules
1. **Keep it simple:** One emotional thread per episode
2. **No exposition:** Show, don't tell backstory
3. **Relatable human emotions:** Jealousy, loneliness, hope, betrayal, forgiveness
4. **Facebook-optimized:** Lines people will quote in comments

### Caption Template
```
[HOOK LINE as first sentence]

[Character name] didn't see this coming...

[2-3 sentence story summary]

[Engagement question]
💬 Have you ever felt like [character]?
👇 Tag someone who needs to see this

#animalsoftiktok #dogsoftiktok #catsoftiktok #dogmom #catmom #pettok
```

## 5. Technical Production Stack

### AI Tools
| Component | Tool | Cost | Notes |
|-----------|------|------|-------|
| Scripts | ChatGPT-4o | $20/mo | Train with character voices, story formulas |
| Video | Kling AI or Pika Labs | ~$0.10-0.30/video | Test both, pick consistent style |
| Voice | ElevenLabs | $5/mo starter | Clone 4 distinct character voices |
| Images (thumbs) | Midjourney/Leonardo | $10-30/mo | Consistent visual style |
| Editing | CapCut | Free | Add captions, music, transitions |

### Workflow (Weekly Batch)
**Sunday (2 hours):**
1. Generate 14 scripts using template prompts
2. Review and refine for emotional beats
3. Create shot lists for each episode

**Monday (3 hours):**
1. Generate all video assets (Kling/Pika)
2. Record all voiceover (ElevenLabs)
3. Rough cut in CapCut

**Tuesday (2 hours):**
1. Final edit all 14 videos
2. Create thumbnails
3. Write captions with engagement hooks
4. Schedule in Meta Business Suite

**Wednesday-Saturday:** Monitor, engage with comments, track metrics

### Prompt Engineering (Character Consistency)
```
Character prompt template:
"[Animal type], [distinctive feature], [clothing/accessory if any], 
[emotion/expression], [setting], 3D animated style, soft lighting, 
Pixar-like quality, 4K, emotional storytelling aesthetic"

Example (Charlie):
"Golden retriever with a blue collar, worried expression, 
sitting by a window at sunset, 3D animated style, soft warm lighting, 
Pixar-like quality, 4K, emotional storytelling aesthetic"
```

## 8. Risk Mitigation & Longevity

### Anti-Spam Protection
1. **Build a page persona:** Have a "voice" in captions, not just video content
2. **Engage genuinely:** Respond to comments like a human creator would
3. **Community features:** Use Facebook Stories, polls, behind-the-scenes
4. **Transparency:** If asked, acknowledge AI-assisted (not AI-only)

### Content Safety Rules
- Never use real animal distress footage
- Always positive or redemptive endings (Facebook algorithm favors positive)
- Avoid political/sensitive topics disguised as animal content
- Keep characters consistent (build recognition, not disposable content)

### Backup Plan
- If Facebook throttles: Instagram Reels (same demographic growing)
- If AI policy changes: Pivot to "illustrated stories" style (less video-dependent)
- If character fatigue: Introduce new cast member, don't replace
