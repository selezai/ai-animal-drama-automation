## Template 1: Betrayal & Loyalty (Charlie's Formula)

### Visual Prompt
```
Golden retriever with blue collar, [emotion progression: hopeful → confused → heartbroken], 
indoor domestic setting with warm lighting, 3D animated style, emotional storytelling aesthetic
```

### Episode Title Format
"Charlie waited by the door every day... until he didn't have to anymore"

### Script Structure (45 seconds)

**0-3s: HOOK (Visual: Charlie looking at camera, tail wagging, then it stops)**
VOICEOVER (warm, earnest):  
*"I thought if I just loved him enough..."*

**3-15s: SETUP**
- Charlie watching owner leave for work
- Sitting by door, waiting
- Flashback: happy moments with owner

VOICEOVER:  
*"Every morning, I'd sit right here. Same spot. For three years. I figured... if I was good, if I was patient, he'd always come back to me."*

**15-30s: CONFLICT/TWIST (Visual: Owner comes home with another dog)**
- Owner enters with puppy
- Charlie's expression changes
- Puppy jumps on owner, gets all the attention

VOICEOVER:  
*"But yesterday... he came home with someone else. And he laughed. He laughed like he used to laugh with me."*

**30-42s: EMOTIONAL PEAK (Visual: Charlie alone in corner, looking at them play)**
VOICEOVER (voice cracks slightly):  
*"I still sit by the door. Old habit, I guess. I just... I don't know who I'm waiting for anymore."*

**42-45s: RESOLUTION (Visual: Milo the cat appears, sits next to Charlie without speaking)**
MILO (dry, but not unkind):  
*"Move over. You're hogging the warm spot."*
[Charlie looks surprised, then almost smiles]

## Template 2: Unexpected Kindness (Milo's Formula)

### Visual Prompt
```
Orange tabby cat with green eyes, [expression: defensive → suspicious → softening], 
alleyway or rooftop setting at dusk, 3D animated style, emotional storytelling aesthetic
```

### Episode Title Format
"They said he had no heart. They were wrong."

### Script Structure (45 seconds)

**0-3s: HOOK (Visual: Milo glaring at camera)**
VOICEOVER (dry, defensive):  
*"I don't do the whole... feelings thing."*

**3-15s: SETUP**
- Milo watching other animals from a distance
- Other animals whispering about him
- Milo acting like he doesn't care

VOICEOVER:  
*"Everyone thinks I'm mean. Rude cat, they say. Whatever. I don't need them. I don't need anyone. I have my spot, my sunbeam, my routine."*

**15-30s: CONFLICT/TWIST (Visual: Small kitten shivering in rain, trying to get to Milo's spot)**
- Kitten approaches Milo's warm spot
- Milo hisses, kitten flinches but is too cold to leave
- Other animals watch, expect Milo to chase it away

VOICEOVER:  
*"This kitten showed up. Shivering. Pathetic. Everyone expected me to... you know. Be me about it."*

**30-42s: EMOTIONAL PEAK (Visual: Milo shifts over, makes room)**
VOICEOVER (quietly, almost to himself):  
*"It's just... it's warmer when you share it. That's all. Don't make it weird."*

**42-45s: RESOLUTION (Visual: Milo and kitten sharing spot, both asleep)**
[Soft music swell. No dialogue. Just the visual.]

## Template 3: Secrets Revealed (Bella's Formula)

### Visual Prompt
```
African Grey Parrot with distinctive red tail, [expression: knowing → dramatic → guilty], 
cozy indoor setting with warm lighting, 3D animated style, emotional storytelling aesthetic
```

### Episode Title Format
"She knows everything. And she's about to tell."

### Script Structure (45 seconds)

**0-3s: HOOK (Visual: Bella leaning in close to camera)**
VOICEOVER (fast, conspiratorial):  
*"Okay. Okay. You want to know what I saw?"*

**3-15s: SETUP**
- Flashback: Bella watching Duke from afar
- Duke doing something secretive (hiding food, visiting a specific spot)
- Bella piecing it together

VOICEOVER:  
*"Duke — big tough Duke who acts like he hates everyone — I've been watching him. Every Tuesday. Same time. He goes to the old shed behind the house."*

**15-30s: CONFLICT/TWIST (Visual: Bella follows Duke, sees him with a stray puppy)**
- Duke carefully placing food down
- Stray puppy eating while Duke watches protectively
- Duke speaking softly to the puppy

VOICEOVER:  
*"And yesterday... I followed him. He's been feeding this stray. For months. Talking to it. Gentle. Like... like he's a completely different dog."*

**30-42s: EMOTIONAL PEAK (Visual: Bella caught by Duke, they stare at each other)**
DUKE (gravelly, threatening but vulnerable):  
*"You tell anyone..."*

BELLA (surprisingly soft):  
*"I won't. I... I get it, Duke. I do."*

**42-45s: RESOLUTION (Visual: Bella flying away, looking back with new understanding)**
VOICEOVER:  
*"Maybe... maybe we're all hiding something soft."

## Template 4: Jealousy & Insecurity (Mixed Character)

### Visual Prompt
```
[Character A] with [distinctive feature], [emotion: content → confused → jealous → realization], 
[setting], 3D animated style, emotional storytelling aesthetic
```

### Episode Title Format
"She thought she was enough. Then the kitten came home."

### Script Structure (45 seconds)

**0-3s: HOOK (Visual: Cat watching owner with new kitten)**
VOICEOVER (uncertain):  
*"I used to be the baby."*

**3-15s: SETUP**
- Flashback: Cat as center of attention
- Owner giving treats, calling her "my baby"
- Happy memories

VOICEOVER:  
*"For four years, I was the baby. 'Who's my baby?' she'd say. And I'd purr. Because I was. I was the baby."*

**15-30s: CONFLICT/TWIST (Visual: Owner brings home kitten, all attention shifts)**
- Kitten gets the premium food
- Kitten sleeps in the favorite spot
- Owner calling kitten "my new baby"

VOICEOVER:  
*"Then she brought home... this. Tiny. Loud. Gets the good food. The spot by the window. And now I'm just... the cat."*

**30-42s: EMOTIONAL PEAK (Visual: Cat hiding, watching them; kitten approaches her)**
KITTEN (small, innocent):  
*"You're the big sister, right? Mom says you're the best. She says to be like you."*

VOICEOVER (cat, voice breaking):  
*"She... she said that?"*

**42-45s: RESOLUTION (Visual: Cat and kitten curled up together)**
VOICEOVER:  
*"Maybe... maybe there's room for two babies."*

## Template 5: Found Family (Duke's Formula)

### Visual Prompt
```
Bulldog with scar above eye, [expression: guarded → suspicious → protective → peaceful], 
outdoor setting transitioning to cozy indoor, 3D animated style, emotional storytelling aesthetic
```

### Episode Title Format
"Everyone gave up on him. Then he found his pack."

### Script Structure (45 seconds)

**0-3s: HOOK (Visual: Duke alone, looking tough)**
VOICEOVER (gravelly, short):  
*"Don't need anyone."*

**3-15s: SETUP**
- Duke's backstory hinted (returned to shelter, scar explanation)
- Other animals avoiding him
- Duke pretending he prefers being alone

VOICEOVER:  
*"Been returned twice. 'Too aggressive,' they said. 'Too much.' I learned. Don't get close. Don't expect... anything."*

**15-30s: CONFLICT/TWIST (Visual: Small scared animal appears, Duke tries to ignore)**
- Scared puppy or kitten appears
- Other animals won't help
- Duke watching, conflicted

VOICEOVER:  
*"This puppy showed up. Lost. Shaking. Everyone else walked by. I told myself... not my problem."*

**30-42s: EMOTIONAL PEAK (Visual: Duke positions himself between puppy and danger/threat)**
- Duke protecting puppy without being asked
- Other animals watching, surprised

VOICEOVER (softer):  
*"But... I know what it's like. Being scared. Being alone. I couldn't just... walk by."*

**42-45s: RESOLUTION (Visual: Charlie, Milo, Bella appear, forming circle around Duke and puppy)**
BELLA:  
*"Well, well. Look who has a soft spot."

DUKE:  
*"Shut up, bird."

[All of them together, new family formed]

## ChatGPT Prompt for Script Generation

Use this prompt to generate new scripts:

```
Write a 45-second AI animal drama script for Facebook following this exact structure:

CHARACTER: [Choose: Charlie (golden retriever, loyal/earnest), Milo (tabby cat, sarcastic/secretly soft), Bella (parrot, gossipy/dramatic), Duke (bulldog, gruff/protective)]

PILLAR: [Choose: Betrayal & Loyalty / Unexpected Kindness / Secrets Revealed / Jealousy & Insecurity / Found Family]

STRUCTURE:
- 0-3s: Hook with emotional opening line
- 3-15s: Setup showing character's hope/dream
- 15-30s: Conflict/twist that changes everything
- 30-42s: Emotional peak with character reaction
- 42-45s: Resolution or cliffhanger

VOICE NOTES:
- Charlie: Warm, earnest, slightly naive, says "I just..." when emotional
- Milo: Dry, witty, defensive, short sentences, "whatever" as verbal tic
- Bella: Fast-talking, dramatic, nosy, uses "okay okay" when excited
- Duke: Gravelly, short sentences, emotional vulnerability in pauses

THEME: [Specific emotion: e.g., "being replaced by someone new" / "finding out someone cares" / "protecting someone weaker"]

Include stage directions in [brackets]. Keep dialogue natural and emotional. One clear emotional thread only.
```
