## AI Content Policy: What Meta Actually Says

### ✅ AI-Generated Content IS Allowed

From Meta's official policy (March 2026):
> "Content filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page is considered original content on Facebook."

**Key point:** Using AI tools (like Luma, ElevenLabs) to CREATE content is treated the same as using a camera or editing software. The content is yours because you prompted it, directed it, and published it.

## AI Disclosure Requirements

### Current Policy (2026)

Meta requires AI disclosure labels for:
- **Realistic deepfakes** of real people
- **Manipulated media** that could deceive about real events
- **Political content** using AI-generated imagery

Meta does NOT require disclosure for:
- **Clearly fictional content** (animated animals)
- **Entertainment/artistic content** that isn't trying to deceive
- **AI-assisted editing** (color correction, effects, etc.)

### Recommendation for Your Content

**You don't need to label your content as AI-generated**, but you CAN choose to:

**Option A: No disclosure (allowed)**
- Your content is clearly animated/fictional
- No one will mistake talking cartoon animals for real footage

**Option B: Voluntary disclosure (recommended)**
- Add "Created with AI" in your bio or video description
- Builds trust with audience
- Protects you if policies tighten
- Some viewers find AI content interesting/novel

**Suggested bio text:**
> "🐕 Daily animal drama stories | Created with AI | Not real animals, just real emotions"

Don't use these phrases in captions:
- "Like if you agree"
- "Share to win"
- "Comment YES if..."
- "Tag someone who..."

Instead, use natural engagement prompts:
- "What would you do in Charlie's situation?"
- "Team Milo or Team Bella?"
- "This hit different 😢"

## Music & Audio Compliance

### The Risk

Using copyrighted music = instant demonetization of that video.

### Solutions

1. **No background music** — Voiceover only (simplest)
2. **Meta's Sound Collection** — Free licensed music in Creator Studio
3. **Royalty-free libraries** — Epidemic Sound, Artlist (paid)
4. **AI-generated music** — Suno, Udio (check their commercial licenses)

### Recommendation

Start with **no background music**. Your voiceover + video is enough. Add music later once you're monetized and can afford proper licensing.

## South Africa Specific Notes

### Monetization Status

South Africa **IS** in the list of eligible countries for Facebook monetization. However:

1. **Verify in Creator Studio** — Go to Monetization → Eligibility
2. **Payout method** — PayPal or bank transfer available
3. **Currency** — Payouts in USD, converted to ZAR
4. **Tax** — You may need to provide tax info to Meta

### If You See "Ineligible Country"

Common causes:
- VPN active (turn it off)
- Page location set incorrectly
- Account created with different country
- Beta rollout not reached your account yet

Fix: Go to Page Settings → Page Info → Verify location is South Africa

## What Happens If You Get Flagged

### "Limited Originality" Flag

**Cause:** System thinks your content is repetitive or template-based.

**Fix:**
1. Vary your story pillars (don't do betrayal every day)
2. Change visual styles occasionally
3. Use different character combinations
4. Appeal in Creator Studio if you believe it's wrong

### "Unoriginal Content" Flag

**Cause:** System detected similarity to other content.

**Fix:**
1. Ensure your prompts are unique
2. Don't reuse the same visual descriptions
3. Appeal with explanation that you created it with AI tools

### Demonetization

**Cause:** Policy violation on specific video or pattern of violations.

**Fix:**
1. Remove the offending video
2. Wait for review period (usually 30 days)
3. Appeal if you believe it's wrong
4. Don't repeat the violation
